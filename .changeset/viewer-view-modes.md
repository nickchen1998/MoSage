Viewer: show several pages at once, as two-up spreads or a grid.

- A view-mode control beside the zoom buttons switches between one continuous column (the default), facing pages that open on a right-hand page, and a zoomed-out grid that fits as many sheets per row as the pane holds.
- The mode is remembered per document. Zoom, fit, page jump, the outline and find work in every mode, and the page counter reports the page you jumped to even when it shares a row.
- Zooming or switching modes keeps the page you are reading in view, and a page zoomed wider than the pane can be scrolled to its left edge.
